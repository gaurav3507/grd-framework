# D4 pre-registration (19 Oct 2026): E3 real-data numbers redone on the held-out basis

Committed before any D4 code. Triggered by E12 (notes/e12_prereg.md, decision D4): the E3 construction fits the PCA basis on all control cells and draws the null from the same cells; on pure-control fakes drawn outside that set it selects 24 to 48 of 50 in K562 and RPE1 (E12 T0i; Norman 0 to 2). Every E3 real-data number computed on that construction is void for the manuscript. This file fixes what replaces each one.

## The held-out construction (E12's, reused verbatim)
Per dataset and seed s in {0, 1, 2}, d = 10: e12_common.control_split(n_controls, s) gives BASIS, REF and FAKE pool; fit_projection(Xc[BASIS], d) gives (mu_h, P_h); environments and REF are projected with project(); where a perturbation's target gene is a feature column j, the environment and a per-environment copy of REF are target-corrected with correct() (e12_common.target_map; run_environment lines 283 to 285). Environments with n_e > n_REF // 2 are excluded (E12: RPE1 ENSG00000108064, Norman KLF1; retained 385, 145, 100). Fakes are 50 draws of fake_n = median n_e rows from the FAKE pool (stream K_FAKE_ROWS; fake_n is stored per seed in results["fake_n"]: K562 270, RPE1 262, Norman 516). The GRD gate is T0h: e12_common.grd against the full corrected REF, disjoint null, B = 9999, stream K_NULL. BH at q = 0.05 over the family of retained real environments plus the 50 fakes, in file order, with precision_readout.bh_fdr exactly as e12_aggregate.family does. Per-environment BH flags are not stored, so they are recomputed from the stored T0h p-values and asserted equal to summary.json's bh_selected_real labels per seed before use. No gate statistic is recomputed. HCP is out (D5). ABIDE (13/13 sites, same in-sample construction) is out with it: the fMRI section is removed. The E3 positive control (synthetic, Fig 5) and E3p0 are not real-data numbers and are untouched.

Context: E13 found the RPE1 T0h A0 rejection rate at alpha 0.05 to be 0.017, below the calibration band (conservative, not anti-conservative). D4 uses the BH decisions as E12 made them.

## Predictions (recorded so they can fail)
- P2 (RPE1 confound survives): in at least 2 of 3 seeds, the T0h-detected observed median exceeds the 95th percentile of the 1000 baseline medians.
- P3 (attribution non-rejection): 0 BH subspace rejections in every seed and in the union, for RPE1 and Norman.
- P4 (iLCS): naive fake firing rate 1.0 and calibrated fake firing rate at most 0.15 in every dataset and seed; Jaccard against the held-out T0h set at most 0.10.
- P5 (stability, descriptive, no pass/fail): RPE1 T0h pairwise Jaccard across seeds, expected low (sets of 8 to 14 from 145; 8 labels are common to all three seeds in summary.json).
(P1 is retired with the structured splits.)

## Consequences fixed in advance
- P2 fails: the RPE1 confound finding leaves the paper's contribution list; Fig 3b is dropped.
- P3 fails: C3 changes from "non-rejection" to the rejections found; reported as is, interpreted after discussion.
- P4 fails on the calibrated rate: the iLCS comparison is reported with the observed rate; the claim that size-matched calibration fixes iLCS is withdrawn.
- Every number in the inventory is replaced in the manuscript by its D4 value whatever the predictions do.

No change to d, B, q, NMIN, the exclusion rule, fake_n, the split fractions, the baseline replicate count or the bucket design after the first run; no reuse of any in-sample number as a D4 number; no dropped seeds; scripts and results committed together.
